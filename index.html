questions

good questions.

Can’t see yours? Ask it on WhatsApp or give us a ring.

Where do you ship from?

Every order is packed and dispatched from our Mumbai hub: Aziz Sahab Building, Janta Colony, Near McDonald’s, Jogeshwari East, Mumbai, Maharashtra 400060, India, and shipped to customers in the USA. Tell us your city and state in your enquiry and we’ll confirm shipping with your quote.

How does pricing work?

We quote per enquiry. Send your colourway, saddle type and quantity (or the kits you like) and we’ll reply with a quote on WhatsApp or over a call. Nothing is charged on this website.

Will the saddle bag fit my saddle?

Tell us your saddle type and size when you enquire. We confirm fit with you before you order, so there’s no guesswork.

Can I get a custom colour or size?

Ask us. Put what you have in mind in the message box and we’ll tell you honestly what’s possible before you commit to anything.

How do I order?

Fill in an enquiry form. It opens WhatsApp with your details ready to send. Prefer talking? Call +1 828 690 6688. We take it from there.

What’s in a wellness kit?

Recovery Tools: foam roller, massage ball, resistance bands. Sleep & Unwind: eye mask, aroma diffuser, soft sleep kit. Hydrate & Go: insulated bottle, shaker, cooling towel. Mindful Movement: yoga mat, blocks, stretch strap. Mix and match, we’ll confirm what’s available when we reply.
